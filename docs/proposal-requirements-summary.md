# Proposal Requirements Summary

Extracted from `Pratap_AI_RealEstate_Proposal_1.docx`.

## Product Direction

Create a SaaS platform for real estate sales operations: a Twenty.com-style open CRM foundation, specialized for brokerages and developers, with AI voice calling, automated follow-ups, management analytics, and inventory matching.

## Problems to Solve

- Leads leak across ads, property portals, walk-ins, referrals, and spreadsheets.
- Cold calling is slow, inconsistent, and rarely logged.
- Follow-ups depend on agent memory.
- Management lacks a trustworthy pipeline view.
- Property-to-buyer matching is manual and slow.

## Required Modules

- Lead capture and CRM core.
- Agent workspace.
- Follow-up and messaging engine.
- Hindi + English AI voice calling agent.
- Management analytics.
- Property inventory matching.
- Add-ons later: inbound AI receptionist, speed-to-lead, lead scoring, appointment scheduling, review/referral automation, document assist.

## Proposed Phasing

- **Phase 1:** CRM foundation, lead capture, agent workspace, follow-ups.
- **Phase 2:** AI voice agent, speed-to-lead, analytics.
- **Phase 3:** Inventory matching and add-ons.
